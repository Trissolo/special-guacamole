⚔️ The Single-Roll Resolution System

Instead of making separate rolls to hit and then to defend, this system resolves the outcome using a 1-100 Percentile Roll (d100) against a target's Defense Rating.

How It Works

Every character has an Accuracy score, and every defender has a Defense score.

  1. Subtract Defense from Accuracy to find the Hit Threshold.

  2. Roll a 100-sided die (or generate a random number from 1 to 100).

  3. Compare the roll to the chart below.

[01 -------------- Hit Threshold -------------- 100]
 └─ Critical/Double ─┘ └─ Normal ─┘ └─ Parry ─┘ └─ Miss ─┘

📊 Outcome Breakdown

To map your four desired outcomes (Double Damage, Normal Wound, Parry, Miss), divide the 1-100 range based on the Hit Threshold:

<
Roll Range Outcome Effect
Lowest 10% of Hit Threshold 💥 Double Damage The attacker bypassed armor completely. Deal 2x Damage.
Up to the Hit Threshold 🩸 Normal Wound The attack connects cleanly. Deal 1x Damage minus armor.
Above Threshold up to +20 🛡️ Parry The defender deflected the blow. Deal 0 Damage; defender may get a counter-attack.
Everything Else (High Rolls) 💨 Miss The attacker swung wide or the defender dodged completely. 0 Damage.
Outcome D100 Roll Range Mechanical Description
Double Damage 01 to Critical Limit (or Doubles) The attack hits an opening. Inflict double damage or maximize modifiers.
Normal Wound Up to Hit Threshold The attack beats the target's defense. Deal standard weapon damage.
Parry Above Hit Threshold to Miss Zone The defender deflects the strike. Attenuate or completely block incoming damage.
Miss High Failure Range (e.g., 91–100) The attack is clean avoided or widely off-target. Zero damage is dealt.

🧮 Practical Example

Let's look at a combat scenario between a Hero and a Goblin:
  • Hero's Accuracy: 75
  • Goblin's Defense: 15
  • Hit Threshold: 75 - 15 = 60 (The Hero has a 60% base chance to land a normal wound or better).
Here is how the random roll breaks down for this specific attack:
  • Roll 1 to 6: 💥 Double Damage (Top 10% of the 60 threshold)
  • Roll 7 to 60: 🩸 Normal Wound (Hits successfully)
  • Roll 61 to 80: 🛡️ Parry (Missed the threshold, but within the defender's 20-point parry window)
  • Roll 81 to 100: 💨 Miss (Complete failure to connect)

🛠️ Why This Mechanic Works Well

  • Fast-Paced: Resolving hit, miss, parry, and critical damage in a single calculation keeps combat moving quickly.
  • Dynamic Scaling: If a Hero fights a high-defense boss, the Hit Threshold shrinks, automatically making Parries and Misses much more common without needing extra rules.
